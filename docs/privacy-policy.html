HvacGauge Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

HvacGauge is an offline HVAC field calculator and documentation app published by Billin. This policy explains what the app stores on your device and what limited analytics and subscription data leave the device.

Data stored on your device

Client units, equipment details, addresses, phone numbers, model and serial numbers, diagnostic readings, notes, pricing and your business profile are stored in a local SQLite database on your phone. This information is not uploaded by HvacGauge and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Analytics

HvacGauge uses Amplitude to understand how the app is used. Analytics events may include app opens, the screens and features used, the selected refrigerant and metering type, charge and capacitor status categories, whether a PDF was printed or shared, paywall views, plan selections, purchase outcomes, and app version, device model and a random app-generated device identifier. Analytics events do not include client names, addresses, phone numbers, equipment serial numbers, notes, or your business profile.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are sold through Google Play. RevenueCat processes subscription status and purchase receipts so the app can unlock Pro features. Google Play processes payment details; HvacGauge never receives your full payment card number.

Sharing

Amplitude and RevenueCat process data on our behalf as service providers. We do not sell personal data and do not share it for advertising. The app contains no advertising SDK.

Data retention and deletion

On-device data remains until you delete the record in the app or uninstall it. To request deletion of analytics data associated with your device, email us and we will remove it within 30 days.

Children

HvacGauge is intended for professional HVAC and refrigeration technicians and is not directed to children under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
